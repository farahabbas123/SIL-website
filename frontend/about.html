Built by students who took the first step themselves.

Step Into INTL Law is a student-run initiative helping the next generation of Australians find their way into international law careers, postgraduate study and the global rule of law.

Our values

What we stand for.

Everything we publish — from the jobs board to our tips-and-tricks content — is built around three commitments.

01

Accessibility first

No paywalls, no jargon. Every resource is written so a first-year student and a final-year graduate get equal value from it.

02

An Australian lens

Global opportunities, curated with Australian students' pathways, visas and timelines specifically in mind.

03

Community over gatekeeping

We're run by students, for students — and we want organisations and universities to help us grow the board, not compete with it.

Our story

How Step Into INTL Law came to be.

The problem

A gap nobody was filling

Students passionate about international law kept hitting the same wall: no single, Australian-focused place to find scholarships, internships or graduate roles in the field.

The response

A board, built by the students who needed it

We started collecting the opportunities we wished we'd known about earlier — scholarships, postgraduate programs and graduate roles — and put them in one place.

Today

Growing with the community

We're expanding into in-depth scholarship guides, application tips and a way for organisations and universities to list opportunities directly.
