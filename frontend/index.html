Mission statement

Every step toward the international rule of law starts with knowing there's a step to take.

Step Into INTL Law exists to help students interested in international law step into their dream career, producing professional development, postgraduate study guidance and knowledge content that demystifies international law career paths for the next generation of young Australian leaders.

The organisation was born out of students' uncertainty. Young people are passionate about international law, but many can't find their first step — not for lack of ambition, but for lack of accessible resources, networks and platforms that speak to it from an Australian perspective. Without a place to start, that passion drifts toward more conventional routes.

International law touches every part of our lives — from the sanctity of human rights to the security of nations and the flow of trade in essential goods. Losing young talent to more conventional paths has real consequences for Australia's agency on the global stage. It's time we took the first step.
